# AriClear — Project Description

## What is AriClear?

AriClear is a web application that helps founders, indie hackers, and small agencies answer one critical question about their website: **Can people (and AI) instantly understand what you do?**

It combines two core scores into a single, actionable report:

1. **5-Second Human Clarity Test** — Simulates whether a real visitor can grasp your business, offer, and target customer within five seconds of landing on your site.
2. **AI-SEO Score (GEO Score)** — Measures how well AI models (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, etc.) can extract, classify, and accurately represent your business when users ask relevant questions.

---

## Why AriClear Exists

Search is changing. A growing share of product discovery now happens through AI assistants rather than traditional search engines. Businesses that aren't visible or clearly understood by AI models are losing customers they never even knew existed.

At the same time, most websites fail the basic human clarity test — visitors bounce in seconds because the value proposition is buried, vague, or buried in jargon.

Existing tools either:

- Focus only on traditional SEO (Semrush, Ahrefs) and are priced for enterprises
- Cover only readability (Hemingway, Readable) without any AI-visibility layer
- Address GEO at enterprise price points (AthenaHQ, Goodie AI)

AriClear fills the gap: **affordable, fast, and dual-purpose** — built specifically for solo founders and small teams who need honest, specific, actionable feedback without the enterprise price tag.

## Core Features

| Feature                          | Description                                                      |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Clarity Score**                | 5-second human readability and messaging clarity analysis        |
| **AI-SEO / GEO Score**           | How accurately AI models understand and classify your business   |
| **Specific Feedback**            | Non-generic feedback that references your actual website content |
| **Scan History**                 | Track score changes over time as you improve your site           |
| **Performance Monitoring**       | Website uptime and load performance tracking                     |
| **PDF Reports**                  | Downloadable reports for client delivery or internal sharing     |

## Execution Principles

AriClear is built around three non-negotiable quality commitments:

1. **Brutally honest scoring** — No grade inflation. A 40 means 40.
2. **Specific, contextual feedback** — Every suggestion references the user's actual website copy, not generic best-practice templates.
3. **Speed** — Scans complete in under 10 seconds.

## Purpose of This Project (Claude.ai)

This Claude.ai Project is used as the primary workspace for AriClear's product development. It serves as a running context layer for:

- Shaping and refining product strategy, feature prioritization, and positioning
- Researching the competitive landscape (Geoptie, Rankscale, Otterly, AthenaHQ, etc.)
- Drafting copy, onboarding flows, pricing pages, and marketing messaging
- Exploring technical architecture and implementation decisions
- Iterating on scoring logic, feedback tone, and report structure

All conversations in this project share context about AriClear's goals, target audience, and current development stage, so Claude can give relevant, specific, and consistent input without needing to re-explain the product from scratch.
